# Direction readiness

Use this gate for scene coverage and motion. It is a creative decision gate, not another user-approval step and not a reason to hold unrelated work. A direct request to generate authorizes narrow, source-faithful repairs first. Return one outcome for each affected scene or production block:

- **PASS:** the cards are truthful, playable, physically feasible, timed, and executable with the selected route.
- **REVISE:** the evidence is known and the smallest repair can be authored now. Repair it before motion without asking the user to approve the repair again.
- **BLOCK:** a missing source fact, impossible sightline or action, unsupported audio ownership, unavailable required control, or unresolved user choice prevents truthful execution. Hold only the affected coverage. If the user explicitly requests an experiment with unresolved creative choices, explain the limits and proceed within that scope; keep the failed or unverified verdict. A test does not override unsupported tool inputs or authorize unrelated work.

Do not promote these evidence states into one another:

`saved and re-read` → persistence only; `direction PASS` → authored coverage is ready for frame work; `frame PASS` → inspected composition and actual dimensions are usable; `motion PASS` → observed action, performance, camera, join, and audible result are usable.

## Receive an incoming production

Keep discovery concise. Re-read the named sources and current Kinex records, then add this block to the existing Production Plan rather than starting a shadow document:

```text
Outcome and authority: requested deliverable; source range; approved facts versus proposals
Audience turn: whose uncertainty matters; audience knows before → learns after
Performance engine: each principal's objective; obstacle; current tactic; decisive trigger
Space: floor-map zone; action axis; entrances; occlusions/elevation; proven and unresolved sightlines
Objects and voices: opening custody; required handoff paths; exact speakers; offscreen/device/playback routes
Format and look: exact ratio; structured setting; native camera fields; composition plan; dominant source/direction; colour profile and progression
Coverage and debt: source units → planned shots; repeated setups; held audio routes; timing not read aloud
Evidence state: saved/read back; frames inspected and dimensions verified; motion watched; sound heard
```

Do not paste the whole production history. Preserve stable ids and prior decisions, name only the current debts, and keep private source material out of reusable public examples.

## Test the scene before motion

Review the coverage as a sequence, not as independent prose fields.

1. **Audience and causality:** Name whose uncertainty the scene carries and what changes for the audience. Each shot must expose a cause, assessment, response, or consequence. “Coverage” and provider duration do not motivate a split.
2. **Playable behaviour:** For each active person, identify the objective aimed at a partner, obstacle, current tactic, perceived trigger, listening, visible response, and exit state. Physical business must be an existing task whose interruption or continuation matters; an isolated fork point, glance, or fidget is decoration when nothing triggers it and nothing changes.
3. **Blocking and custody:** Start from first-frame occupancy. For every handled object, preserve starting owner/state → reachable path → contact → release → ending owner/state. Verify that the actor, camera, and audience can actually see across walls, floors, balconies, screens, or occlusions. Mark unsupported space unresolved; do not solve it with confident prose.
4. **Camera and light:** Choose the audience information first, then state the camera's physical zone, same-side relationship to the action axis, height, view, movement, and the complete prop path it must reveal. Repeated frontal two-shots are valid only when the unchanged relationship is the point; otherwise progress point of view, scale, depth, or access with the drama. Positive operator behaviour is required—“no push-in” is not a shot. Establish the motivated light source, direction, camera relation, and colour change; a camera or film-stock name is not colour design.
5. **Timing, edit, and sound:** Establish natural spoken timing with a timed read when available; label textual estimates as unverified. Fit action around speech without rushing words or filling a provider maximum with dead air. Name the incoming hook, the event that earns the cut, and the outgoing state. Preserve real speaker ownership; hold an incompatible multi-speaker, offscreen, device, or playback route instead of assigning every line to one convenient voice.
6. **Format and evidence:** Keep the exact ratio in structured settings as well as prose, and synchronize supported native camera fields instead of leaving revisions only in canvas notes. Exact 21:9 reduces to `7:3`; use an equivalent advertised operation token or an Adaptive source with verified pixels, not an invented enum. Compose for the target before crop. Establish the intended colour profile or display target without claiming unverified output metadata. Inspect the actual frame with captions and diagrams hidden: verify that foreground overlap and scale, visible faces, and background parallax prove the intended camera station. A head turn, crop, or lens label is not a new viewpoint. Also verify anatomical handedness, prop custody, and body-bound accessories across reverses. A prompt, task response, canvas note, caption, or successful field readback is not visual or motion evidence.

## Audit coverage growth

Shot count is a consequence of storytelling and feasibility, never a quality score. When a revision materially increases coverage, compare the old and new plans by source unit and report:

- which added shots are earned by an audience-information change, performance turn, necessary action phase, point of view, feasibility repair, or source transition;
- consecutive setups that repeat the same view without a relationship change;
- dialogue split across clips, unverified natural timing, and new multi-speaker or playback routes;
- connector shots or holds that exist only to satisfy a provider duration;
- the smallest merges, camera changes, or audio holds that remove debt without dropping source events.

Do not reject a plan from its ratio alone. A large split can be correct; every added unit must still earn its cut and viable sound route.

## Compact card evidence

Keep the four normal lanes. Ensure they answer these questions without inventing unsupported schema fields:

```text
Identity/assets — active cast and variants; exact copy; opening knowledge; prop and voice ownership
Direction — purpose; objective/tactic; trigger → listening → response; task; full prop path; exit state
Camera — feasible zone/side/axis; composition; operator behaviour; decisive information made legible; motivated light
Edit — natural timing; incoming hook; cut motivation; outgoing state; neighbouring dependency; audio route
```

A fail example is three consecutive frontal two-shots split only to fill clip duration, with a decorative prop point, unverified sightline, negative-only camera notes, and dialogue assigned to one speaker. Renaming those frames “south,” “south-west,” or “OTS” does not repair them when the uncaptioned images preserve the same foreground, faces, scale, and background. A pass example may use fewer shots: establish the task and geography, cut when one person perceives the decisive cue, stay on the listener through assessment, and exit on the changed object or relationship state. The repository's structured fixtures lint review records for omissions and contradictions. They do not judge raw creative work, inspect media, prove that declared evidence exists, or grant a production PASS. Review the actual source, card, prompt and media for those decisions.
